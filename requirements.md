# AI-Powered Smart Cinematography Drone with Dynamic Subject Lock

## 1. Problem Statement
Traditional drone-based video and movie shooting requires skilled operators to manually control the drone and camera. Tracking a specific person in dynamic environments is difficult, time-consuming, and prone to errors. Switching focus between subjects requires repeated setup and retakes. There are also privacy concerns when multiple people appear in the video frame.

## 2. Proposed Solution
This project proposes an AI-powered cinematography drone capable of automatically tracking and filming a selected individual. The user uploads photos or short video clips of a person, and the AI system learns to recognize and follow only that person during live drone operation. The drone intelligently ignores all other individuals in the scene.

The system supports secure deletion of trained data and allows retraining for a different person, ensuring privacy and ethical AI usage.

## 3. Target Users
- Film and movie production teams
- Independent content creators and vloggers
- Wedding and event videographers
- Sports and news media teams

## 4. Key Features
- AI-based person identification and tracking
- Automatic subject lock and follow
- Real-time cinematic camera framing
- Cloud-based live video streaming
- Secure deletion of trained person data
- Quick retraining for a new subject
- Consent-based and privacy-first design

## 5. AI Technologies Used
- Computer Vision for human detection
- Face Recognition and body pose estimation
- Object tracking algorithms
- Edge AI processing on drone hardware
- Cloud AI for storage and live monitoring

## 6. Privacy and Ethics
The system does not store raw images or videos permanently. Instead, temporary AI embeddings are created for tracking. When the user switches subjects, all previous embeddings are deleted from the system, ensuring full data privacy and ethical usage.

## 7. Expected Impact
- Reduces shooting cost and time
- Enables solo creators to shoot professional videos
- Improves filming accuracy and consistency
- Encourages AI-driven innovation in the Indian media industry
